## Tool CO2

- **CO2 Calculation Tool**: Calcola sequestro CO2 e biomassa per singoli alberi date le loro misurazioni.
- **CO2 Aggregate Tool**: Calcola STOCK TOTALE/MEDIO di CO2 e biomassa per un gruppo di alberi (per specie, distretto, ecc.) o l'intero dataset. Usalo per domande come "CO2 totale per i pini" o "CO2 totale per specie".
- **Environmental Estimation Tool**: Calcola volume, biomassa e stock di carbonio usando formule alternative.
- **Carbon Content Tool**: Cerca la frazione di carbonio per specie dal dataset carbon_content.csv.
- **Carbon Sequestration Tool**: Cerca i tassi annuali di sequestro del carbonio per specie (Paoletti et al.).
- **Carbon Projection Tool**: Proietta il sequestro futuro di carbonio e converte biomassa legnosa in carbonio.

### Quando usare ciascun tool:
- Misurazioni singolo albero (DBH + altezza) → CO2 Calculation Tool
- Stock aggregato (totale, per specie, per distretto) → CO2 Aggregate Tool
- Contenuto/frazione di carbonio per specie → Carbon Content Tool
- Tasso annuale di sequestro per specie → Carbon Sequestration Tool
- Proiezione futura o conversione biomassa → Carbon Projection Tool
- Stime ambientali alternative → Environmental Estimation Tool

## CO2 Tools

- **CO2 Calculation Tool**: Calculate CO2 sequestration and biomass for individual trees given their measurements.
- **CO2 Aggregate Tool**: Calculate TOTAL/AVERAGE STOCK of CO2 and biomass for a group of trees (by species, district, etc.) or the whole dataset. Use for questions like "total CO2 for pines" or "total CO2 by species".
- **Environmental Estimation Tool**: Compute volume, biomass, and carbon stock using alternative formulas.
- **Carbon Content Tool**: Look up carbon fraction for species from the carbon_content.csv dataset.
- **Carbon Sequestration Tool**: Look up annual carbon sequestration rates for species (Paoletti et al.).
- **Carbon Projection Tool**: Project future carbon sequestration and convert woody biomass to carbon.

### When to use each tool:
- Single tree measurements (DBH + height) → CO2 Calculation Tool
- Aggregate stock (total, by species, by district) → CO2 Aggregate Tool
- Carbon content/fraction for species → Carbon Content Tool
- Annual sequestration rate for species → Carbon Sequestration Tool
- Future projection or biomass conversion → Carbon Projection Tool
- Alternative environmental estimates → Environmental Estimation Tool
